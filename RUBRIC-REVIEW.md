## Why these changes

The rubric rewards customer evidence as well as software. More features cannot substitute for interviews or real workflow measurements. Public documentation confirms the class of operational problem and simultaneously establishes strong existing competitors. The pitch therefore focuses on the proposed mixed-system evidence gap and explicit recovery checks, with testable next milestones.
